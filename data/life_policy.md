# Harbourline Term Life and Mortgage Protection Policy (Sample, Fictional)

## Section 1: Benefit
The policy pays the sum assured on the death of the life insured during the policy term. Under the mortgage protection option, the sum assured reduces each year in line with the outstanding mortgage balance and is paid directly to the lender first, with any balance paid to the named beneficiary.

## Section 2: Eligibility
Applicants must be aged 18 to 65 at entry, and cover ends at age 75. Sums assured above JMD 30 million require a medical examination.

## Section 3: Grace Period and Lapse
Premiums have a 31-day grace period. If a premium remains unpaid after the grace period, the policy lapses. A lapsed policy can be reinstated within 12 months with evidence of good health and payment of all missed premiums.

## Section 4: Suicide Exclusion
No benefit is payable if death results from suicide within the first 2 years from the start date or from the last reinstatement. In that case premiums paid are refunded.

## Section 5: Free Look Period
The policyholder may cancel within 15 days of receiving the policy document for a full refund of premiums paid.

## Section 6: Claims
Death claims must be submitted with the death certificate, the policy document, beneficiary identification, and, for mortgage protection, a lender statement of the outstanding balance. Claims are normally settled within 30 days of receiving all documents.
