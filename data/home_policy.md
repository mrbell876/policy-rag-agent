# Harbourline Homeowners Policy (Sample, Fictional)

## Section 1: What Is Covered
The policy covers the building and permanent fixtures against fire, lightning, explosion, windstorm, hurricane, earthquake, flood, burst pipes and malicious damage. Contents cover is optional and must be shown on the schedule.

## Section 2: Catastrophe Deductible
Losses caused by hurricane, windstorm, earthquake or flood are subject to a catastrophe deductible of 2% of the building sum insured. All other losses carry a standard deductible of JMD 25,000.

## Section 3: Underinsurance (Average Clause)
If the sum insured is less than 85% of the replacement cost of the building at the time of loss, the claim payment is reduced in proportion to the underinsurance. Policyholders should review the sum insured every year.

## Section 4: Unoccupancy
Cover for theft, malicious damage and burst pipes is suspended if the home is left unoccupied for more than 60 consecutive days, unless the insurer agrees in writing.

## Section 5: Making a Claim
Losses must be reported within 14 days, or within 30 days after a declared catastrophe event. The insured must take reasonable steps to prevent further damage, keep damaged items for inspection, and provide photographs and repair quotations.

## Section 6: Alternative Accommodation
If the home becomes uninhabitable because of an insured loss, the policy pays reasonable alternative accommodation costs up to 10% of the building sum insured, for a maximum of 6 months.
