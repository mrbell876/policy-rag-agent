# Harbourline Private Motor Policy (Sample, Fictional)

## Section 1: Cover Types
The policy is offered as Third Party Only, Third Party Fire and Theft, or Comprehensive. Comprehensive cover pays for accidental damage to the insured vehicle in addition to third party liability, fire and theft.

## Section 2: Excess
A standard excess of 10% of the claim amount, minimum JMD 50,000, applies to every own-damage claim. Drivers under 25 years old or with less than two years of driving experience pay an additional young driver excess of JMD 40,000. Windscreen claims carry no excess when repaired at an approved glass vendor.

## Section 3: No Claims Discount
A no claims discount of 10% is earned for each claim-free year, up to a maximum of 50% after five years. One own-damage claim reduces the discount by 20 percentage points. Windscreen claims do not affect the no claims discount.

## Section 4: Making a Claim
Accidents must be reported within 7 days. The insured must submit a completed claim form, a copy of the driver's licence, the police report for any accident involving a third party or injury, and two repair estimates. The insured must not authorise repairs before the claims officer approves an estimate.

## Section 5: Exclusions
The policy does not cover wear and tear, mechanical or electrical breakdown, damage while the vehicle is used for racing or hire and reward without endorsement, or any loss while the driver is under the influence of alcohol or drugs. Unlicensed drivers are not covered.

## Section 6: Courtesy Car
Comprehensive policies include a courtesy car for up to 10 days while the insured vehicle is being repaired at an approved garage.
